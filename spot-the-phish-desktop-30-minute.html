How to play

You play Alex Morgan at Northstar Works. Investigate each email, explain your decision, and learn from the ones that fool you.

30 minutes for 6 emails. About 5 minutes per message, including feedback. The clock starts at Play and continues if you close or reload the game.
1
Inspect before you act.

Open sender headers, hover over links, or use Inspect evidence. That panel reveals destinations and file metadata without visiting sites or opening files.

2
Check a trusted source.

Use the office briefing to check domains, contacts, and expected requests. Don’t click email buttons or attachments to investigate. On a phone, use the inspection panel.

3
Give a reason. Make your call.

Write a short reason, then choose phish or legit. Earn 100 points per correct answer. Clicking phishing content costs 25 points per message, capped once. Nothing harmful opens here.

4
Learn, then save your results.

Read the evidence after every answer. Your final debrief includes your name, reasoning, clicks, score, and an offline verification signature.

Enter at least your first and last name (two name parts). Your name stays in this browser and your downloaded report.
